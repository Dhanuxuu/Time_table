Cutting down social media without quitting it

Give it two fixed windows: the commute home and 20 minutes before bed.About 1.5 hours a day, on purpose, instead of several hours by accident.
Set app timers on your phone for 30 minutes per app.When it locks, that is your signal to stop, not a reason to override it.
During Blocks 1 and 2, the phone goes in another room, or on Do Not Disturb.Most scrolling starts with a quick check during work, so remove the check.
Move the apps off your home screen, log out after each session, and turn off notifications.A few extra taps is enough to break the automatic habit.
Charge your phone outside the bedroom and use a normal alarm clock.This protects your 22:00 sleep, which protects your memory and energy.

Priority order

1. FYP (IoT honeynet) comes first, every day.Block 1 is always FYP until it is submitted.
2. HTB SOC path is Block 2.It also helps your SOC job directly.

5. Movies and dramas on your off day only.Weekday evenings belong to Blocks 1 and 2.
